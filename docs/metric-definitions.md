# Metric Definitions — Enterprise ACV & Revenue Intelligence

## ACV
**Annual Contract Value (ACV)** represents the normalized annual recurring value associated with an active contract or recurring commercial agreement.

## GACV
**Growth ACV (GACV)** represents the net recurring-value movement associated with renewal, expansion, contraction, and loss activity under the governed business definition.

## Bookings
Bookings represent approved commercial value recognized as booked within the selected reporting period.

## Expiring ACV
Recurring value scheduled to reach contract expiration within the selected period or renewal window.

## Renewal Movement
The change between expiring recurring value and renewed recurring value, classified into flat renewal, expansion, contraction, or non-renewal.

## Weighted Contract Term
A contract-duration metric weighted by the approved financial basis, such as ACV or booking value, rather than a simple arithmetic average.

## Geographic Performance
ACV / bookings / expirations grouped by governed customer or contract geography according to the documented mapping hierarchy.

## FX-Adjusted Value
A local-currency financial value translated into the reporting currency using the approved rate and period convention.

> Public examples use synthetic values; exact enterprise definitions are intentionally generalized.
